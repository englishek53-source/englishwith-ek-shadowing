Stop Comparing Yourself to Others

📚 Vocabulary

WordMeaning

JourneyThe process of reaching a goal
InvisibleImpossible to see
StruggleA difficult experience or challenge
AchievementSomething you successfully complete
ProgressImprovement over time
UniqueDifferent from everything else
MeaningfulImportant and valuable
